01 The shift

Ten blue links became one AI answer. The game changed. I'm playing the new one.

SEO → GEO

Rankings still matter. But answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity now decide who gets cited. I study how they pick sources and shape content to be the one they quote.

Built with AI, not just using it

Python, Claude API and React tools that cluster keywords, write briefs, check compliance and localize content for 20+ markets. Robots do the repetitive work; people do the thinking.

Performance DNA

Ten years of paid search, CRO and analytics means every idea meets a number. If it doesn't move bookings, sales or ROAS, it's a hobby. (I have hobbies too. They're also measured.)
